Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy explains how the game Munch Blocks (Android, package com.ctknight.munchblocks), published by Cagri Tekmen (CT Knight), handles your data.

Data we collect

We do not collect any personal data. There is no account and no sign-in. Your game progress (levels, score, gems, Munchers, village and settings) is stored only on your device. If you uninstall the game, this data is deleted. We do not run our own servers for the game and do not receive your progress.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads from Google AdMob, including optional reward videos. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This is done by Google under its own policy: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Your consent and choices

  • In the EU/EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown (Google's consent form). You can accept, refuse, or manage options.
  • In US states with privacy laws, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information.
  • You can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings → Privacy choices.
  • You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Privacy → Ads).

If you refuse consent, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalised.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through ads in the game, please contact us.

Permissions

The game uses internet access only to load ads and the consent form, and vibration for game feedback (can be turned off in Settings). It does not access your contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera.

Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always on this page, with the date above.
